# Krina & Mayur — Wedding Invitation Website

A luxury, interactive wedding invitation website built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.

## ✨ Features

- **Preloader Screen**: Animated Ganesha idol motif with progress loading bar.
- **Intro Video Curtain**: Fullscreen cinematic video overlay with "Tap to Open Invitation" button and smooth transition.
- **Background Music Player**: Floating glassmorphic toggle at bottom-right with mute/unmute control.
- **Floating Petals Animation**: Continuous falling rose & marigold petals across the viewport.
- **Hero Banner & Blessings**: Decorative cherry blossom corners, swaying lanterns, and traditional Ganesha invocation.
- **Interactive Live Countdown**: Dynamic countdown timer to the wedding day with framed couple photo.
- **Events Schedule**: 6-event celebration itinerary with date, time, venue, and direct Google Maps directions.
- **Interactive RSVP**:
  - Quick attendance filter presets (All Events, Day 1, Day 2, Wedding Day, Decline)
  - Custom event attendance checkboxes
  - Team Bride / Team Groom selection
  - Guest count & hair/makeup styling requests
  - Personal blessings message & instant local confirmation card
- **Wardrobe Guide**: Illustrated outfit theme cards for Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding, and After Party.
- **Venue & Google Map**: Embedded interactive map of Koti Resorts, Shimla with directions button.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Optimized for smartphones, tablets, and desktop displays.

## 🚀 Live Preview / Deployment

### GitHub Pages Setup:
1. Go to your repository on GitHub: `https://github.com/mayur0711/krina-mayur-wedding`
2. Click **Settings** > **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment**:
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main** / **(root)**
4. Click **Save**.
5. Your wedding invitation will be live at:
   `https://mayur0711.github.io/krina-mayur-wedding/`
